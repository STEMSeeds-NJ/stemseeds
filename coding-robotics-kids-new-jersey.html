Coding and robotics learning in New Jersey

Explore STEMSeeds' introductory technology pathway for coding, robotics, computational thinking, and digital skills.

What learners can explore

The STEMSeeds Tech Innovators pathway introduces technology as a practical way to solve problems and express ideas. Topics may include introductory coding, robotics, computational thinking, and digital skills. The learning approach emphasizes clear explanations, curiosity, and making connections between instructions, systems, and results.

An opportunity might begin with a question about how a digital tool works, a small coding idea, a design challenge, or a robotics concept. The exact activity, resources, and format depend on the request and should be confirmed directly with STEMSeeds.

Who should contact STEMSeeds?

Students, parents, schools, libraries, community centers, and youth organizations can use the forms page or contact STEMSeeds with a general description of the learning interest. The website does not publish a universal age range, county list, schedule, or availability promise.

Connect technology to other STEM subjects

Coding and robotics can connect with Engineering Builders, Science Explorers, and Math Masters. Read the about page for the wider student-led mission.
