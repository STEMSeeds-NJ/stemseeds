# STEMSeeds

## Student-led STEM tutoring and hands-on learning in New Jersey

STEMSeeds is a student-led STEM education initiative in New Jersey focused on approachable, hands-on learning. It supports learners through peer-to-peer teaching, tutoring, practical exploration, and community connections.

### Programs

- **Science Explorers:** experiments, observation, and scientific thinking.
- **Tech Innovators:** introductory coding, robotics, computational thinking, and digital skills.
- **Engineering Builders:** design challenges, building activities, and problem solving.
- **Math Masters:** mathematical reasoning, foundational concepts, and interactive practice.
