About STEMSeeds

Planting the seeds for the future through STEM education

STEMSeeds is a student-led STEM education initiative in New Jersey founded by Nandish Panchal, Mahi Patel, and Dviti Soni.

Our approach

We make science, technology, engineering, and math approachable through peer-to-peer teaching, tutoring, practical exploration, and hands-on activities. Our goal is to help learners build confidence while discovering ideas that feel useful and exciting.

Students and parents can explore current learning opportunities through the forms page. Schools, libraries, community centers, and youth organizations can contact STEMSeeds to discuss a possible learning connection.

What we value

We value welcoming spaces, clear explanations, curiosity, and learning by doing. STEMSeeds does not promise a particular academic result; each opportunity is shaped around the learner's needs and the information available when a request is submitted.
