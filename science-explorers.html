STEMSeeds program

Science Explorers

Hands-on experiments, scientific thinking, observation, and discovery that bring science to life.

What this pathway is about

Science Explorers is a STEMSeeds learning pathway for curious learners who want to investigate how the world works. The emphasis is on asking questions, noticing evidence, explaining observations, and connecting ideas to practical activities. STEMSeeds is student-led, so the learning experience is designed to feel approachable and collaborative rather than intimidating.

Activities can begin with a question and develop through observation, comparison, discussion, and reflection. A learner might explore a scientific idea through a demonstration, a guided activity, or a conversation about an everyday example. The exact activity depends on the request and the opportunity available.

How to get started

Use the STEMSeeds forms page to share the learner's interests and the kind of support that would be useful. Families can also contact STEMSeeds with a general question. Please share only information needed to help us understand the request.

Related pathways

Some learners may also enjoy Tech Innovators, Engineering Builders, or Math Masters. Read the about page for more context on the student-led approach.
