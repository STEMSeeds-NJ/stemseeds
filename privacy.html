Privacy

Information shared with STEMSeeds

This page explains the practical privacy guidance for using the STEMSeeds website and forms.

What you choose to share

The website does not require an account to browse. The forms page links to Google Forms operated through Google. Information submitted there may include contact details, learning interests, and information needed to respond to a request. Please provide only what is necessary and do not submit passwords, financial information, medical details, or other sensitive information.

How information is used

STEMSeeds uses information that you voluntarily provide to understand a learning request, respond to an inquiry, coordinate a possible tutoring opportunity, or discuss an educational collaboration. We do not promise a placement or a particular academic outcome. Google may process form data under its own policies and terms.

Questions or updates

For privacy questions or to ask about information you submitted, email admin@stemseeds.org. Include enough context to help us locate the request, but do not send unnecessary personal information in the email.
