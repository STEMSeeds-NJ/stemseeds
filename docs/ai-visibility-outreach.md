# AI visibility outreach checklist

This checklist covers work that requires an authorized STEMSeeds representative. Nothing on this page claims that a profile, review, partnership, or press mention already exists.

## Verified organization profile work

- Confirm the official organization name, address, email, logo, social URLs, and any public phone number.
- Claim and complete the organization's profiles on Candid/GuideStar, GreatNonprofits, VolunteerMatch, and Idealist if eligible.
- Create or verify a Google Business Profile only if STEMSeeds has an appropriate public-facing location and can meet Google's eligibility rules.
- Keep the name, address, and contact information consistent with [contact.html](../contact.html).

## Citable community evidence

- Ask confirmed school, library, or community partners whether they can list a real STEMSeeds activity on their official event calendar or organization page.
- Offer local reporters a factual story about the founders and a specific completed activity; do not pitch an unverified impact claim.
- Participate in New Jersey parent and education communities only when a real question can be answered helpfully and the STEMSeeds affiliation is disclosed.
- Never create synthetic reviews, undisclosed promotional comments, or directory listings that imply an unconfirmed partnership.

## Testimonials and reviews

- Request written permission before publishing a parent, learner, teacher, or partner quote.
- Record the reviewer's role and the date of permission.
- Publish only the exact approved wording and avoid claims about outcomes that cannot be substantiated.

## Site maintenance

- Publish an update only after the activity, date, and description have been verified.
- Add a visible date and appropriate `Article` or `BlogPosting` JSON-LD only when a real update exists.
- Recheck the sitemap and canonical URL after adding a public page.
