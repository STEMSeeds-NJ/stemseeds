About STEMSeeds

Learning should feel welcoming, useful, and possible to explore.

Our approach

STEMSeeds is a student-led initiative founded by three high school students who want to make STEM learning engaging and accessible. We design approachable activities and learning opportunities that encourage curiosity and confidence.

Our work centers on peer-to-peer teaching, practical exploration, and community connections. Start with a program, learn more about the team, or use the forms to share what you are looking for.

  • Hands-on learning
    Explore ideas through activities and experiments.
  • Student-led teaching
    Learn with peers who understand the learner experience.
  • Welcoming spaces
    Build confidence while discovering new interests.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to help you get oriented.

What is STEMSeeds?
STEMSeeds is a student-led STEM education initiative in New Jersey focused on approachable, hands-on learning.
Who started STEMSeeds?
STEMSeeds was founded by Nandish Panchal, Mahi Patel, and Dviti Soni.
What types of STEM learning does STEMSeeds offer?
STEMSeeds focuses on student-led teaching, hands-on activities, STEM tutoring, and learning opportunities.
What programs does STEMSeeds focus on?
The current categories are Science Explorers, Tech Innovators, Engineering Builders, and Math Masters.
How do I sign up?
Review the programs and use the forms page to share your interests.
How do I request tutoring?
Use the Free Tutoring Session Form on the forms page.
Can a school or community organization contact STEMSeeds?
Yes. Schools, libraries, community centers, youth organizations, and other educational or community organizations can email stemseeds.nj@gmail.com.
How can I contact STEMSeeds?
Email stemseeds.nj@gmail.com or use the social links below.
Where can I see STEMSeeds projects and activities?
Follow STEMSeeds on Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn.
