About STEMSeeds

Learning should feel welcoming, useful, and possible to explore.

Our approach

STEMSeeds is a student-led STEM education initiative in New Jersey founded by three high school students who want to make science, technology, engineering, and math engaging and accessible. We provide STEM tutoring and hands-on learning opportunities that encourage curiosity and confidence.

Our work centers on peer-to-peer teaching and practical exploration. Explore the programs, learn about the team, or use the forms page to share what you are looking for.

  • Hands-on learning
    Explore ideas through activities and experiments.
  • Student-led teaching
    Learn with peers who understand the learner experience.
  • Welcoming spaces
    Build confidence while discovering new interests.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to help you get oriented. Browse the FAQ or visit the forms page to take the next step.

What is STEMSeeds?
STEMSeeds is a student-led STEM education initiative in New Jersey focused on approachable, hands-on learning.
Does STEMSeeds offer STEM tutoring in New Jersey?
STEMSeeds offers student-led STEM tutoring and learning opportunities in New Jersey. Use the forms page to share what kind of support would be useful.
What STEM subjects does STEMSeeds cover?
STEMSeeds covers science, technology, engineering, and math through tutoring, hands-on activities, and learning opportunities.
Who leads STEMSeeds?
STEMSeeds was founded by Nandish Panchal, Mahi Patel, and Dviti Soni.
How can I request a tutoring session?
Use the Free Tutoring Session Form on the STEMSeeds forms page.
Can schools and community organizations work with STEMSeeds?
Schools, libraries, community centers, youth organizations, and other educational or community organizations can email STEMSeeds about learning opportunities or collaboration.
Is STEMSeeds student-led?
Yes. STEMSeeds is a student-led initiative centered on peer-to-peer teaching, practical exploration, and community connections.
What programs does STEMSeeds focus on?
The current categories are Science Explorers, Tech Innovators, Engineering Builders, and Math Masters.
Where can I see STEMSeeds projects and activities?
Follow STEMSeeds on Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn for projects, activities, and updates.
