STEM tutoring in New Jersey

Learn how to ask STEMSeeds about student-led tutoring and hands-on learning opportunities across science, technology, engineering, and math.

What STEMSeeds can help you explore

STEMSeeds is a student-led STEM education initiative in New Jersey. Its work centers on approachable explanations, peer-to-peer teaching, and practical exploration. Learners can explore science, technology, engineering, and math through the four STEMSeeds pathways: Science Explorers, Tech Innovators, Engineering Builders, and Math Masters.

The website does not make a universal promise about price, grade level, county, delivery format, or availability. Those details should be confirmed for the specific request rather than assumed from a search result.

How to request information

Use the STEMSeeds forms page to share the general subject area and learning interest. You can also email STEMSeeds with a general question. Do not submit passwords, financial information, medical details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

For schools and community groups

Schools, libraries, community centers, youth organizations, and other educational or community organizations can describe a possible learning connection through the school partnerships page. Read the about page to understand the student-led approach and the privacy page before sharing information.
