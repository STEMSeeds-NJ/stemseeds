STEMSeeds program

Math Masters

Mathematical reasoning, problem solving, foundational concepts, and interactive practice.

What this pathway is about

Math Masters treats mathematics as a way of thinking, explaining, and solving problems rather than only a list of answers. STEMSeeds creates approachable opportunities to work through foundational concepts, reason about a problem, and practice communicating the steps behind a solution.

Activities may use an everyday situation, a puzzle, an example to compare, or an interactive practice task. A learner can explore more than one way to approach a question and discuss which information matters. The exact topic and activity depend on the request and the opportunity available.

How to get started

Use the STEMSeeds forms page to share the learner's interests and the kind of mathematical support that would be useful. For a school or community opportunity, contact STEMSeeds with a general description. See the privacy page before submitting information.
