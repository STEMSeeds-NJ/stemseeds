Peer-to-peer learning

Student-led peer tutoring in New Jersey

Explore a welcoming STEM learning approach led by students who want to make science, technology, engineering, and math easier to approach.

Why student-led learning?

STEMSeeds is founded and guided by students. Its approach centers on peer-to-peer teaching, practical exploration, and explanations that recognize the learner experience. The aim is to make it easier to ask questions, work through an idea, and build confidence while exploring STEM.

Student-led does not mean that every request has the same format or outcome. The right opportunity depends on the learner's interests and the information available when a request is submitted. Confirm the details directly with STEMSeeds instead of assuming a grade range, county, schedule, or delivery format.

Subjects and pathways

STEMSeeds covers science, technology, engineering, and math through Science Explorers, Tech Innovators, Engineering Builders, and Math Masters.
