STEMSeeds program

Tech Innovators

Introductory coding, robotics, computational thinking, and digital skills for curious learners.

What this pathway is about

Tech Innovators introduces technology as something learners can question, build with, and understand. The pathway brings together introductory coding, robotics, computational thinking, and digital skills in a student-led learning environment.

The focus is on making ideas clear and practical. Learners can explore how instructions become actions, how systems respond to inputs, and how a problem can be broken into smaller steps. Activities may involve code, a design challenge, a robotics concept, or a discussion of how digital tools work. The exact activity depends on the request and the opportunity available.

How to get started

Use the STEMSeeds forms page to share the learner's interests and what kind of technology learning would be helpful. For a school or community question, contact STEMSeeds with a general description of the opportunity. Avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information.

Related pathways

Technology connects naturally with Engineering Builders, Science Explorers, and Math Masters. Read about the STEMSeeds approach before choosing a next step.
