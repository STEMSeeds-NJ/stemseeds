Community connections

School STEM partnerships in New Jersey

Schools, libraries, community centers, and youth organizations can contact STEMSeeds about student-led STEM learning opportunities.

What a conversation can cover

STEMSeeds is a student-led STEM education initiative focused on approachable, hands-on learning. A school or community organization can describe the learners it hopes to support, the STEM subject or idea it has in mind, and the kind of educational connection it is exploring.

Possible topics include science exploration, introductory technology learning, engineering design, and mathematical reasoning. The four program pages explain the current categories: Science Explorers, Tech Innovators, Engineering Builders, and Math Masters.

How to make an inquiry

Send a general partnership question to admin@stemseeds.org or use the contact page. Include the organization type, broad subject area, and the learning opportunity you are considering. Do not send unnecessary student records or sensitive personal information.

Set expectations clearly

The website does not promise a particular schedule, county, delivery format, participant age range, or result. Those details should be discussed directly for each request. Read the about page for context and the privacy page before sharing information.
